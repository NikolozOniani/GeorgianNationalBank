# R3 monetary-policy rule revision, 17 November 2026

Scope covers the Georgian interest-rate equation only. The earlier R3 scenario and the historical outcomes are known. Register this design before fitting the revised equation. The new target is the change in the announced rate at an actual MPC decision. Between decisions the rate carries forward. For a monthly implementation use the effective dates and compound decision changes additively in percentage points.

Use public quarterly NBG report vintages in the 3% target regime (2018–2024). Each decision uses the most recent available report from a strictly earlier month, with age at most four calendar months. Use the printed report month/year since archive record dates can denote migration. Omit and document unreadable reports/decisions. Preserve the prior announced rate including after a dropped observation. Take the report's last completed quarter of actual headline inflation, central inflation forecast four quarters after that observation, and central current-calendar-year real GDP growth forecast. The last variable is an activity outlook with a variable horizon, not an output gap. The inflation forecast is the central bank's conditional expectation. Record source page and calibration for chart-derived values, round to 0.1 percentage point, validate against printed axes and the target. If a series cannot be verified, leave it missing. No same-decision forecast or future policy decision is an input.

Primary specification is OLS of the announced change on intercept, prior rate, actual inflation minus 3%, expected inflation minus 3%, and expected real growth. Omit lagged intervention and realised same-month global prices. No sign constraints, tuning, respecification or clipping. Compare with no change, last decision change, and nested rule omitting forecast inflation. Fit through 2022 then expand through each previous decision in 2023–24. Report common-sample count, RMSE and MAE in percentage points, direction and annual errors. Also fit once through 2022 as a fixed-coefficient check. Economic diagnostics include the conditional equilibrium under rate smoothing, forecast-inflation and activity response signs, and deleting one entire training year at a time. Require lower pooled expanding RMSE than no change, stable own-rate dynamics and a positive forward inflation response across deletion fits for adoption as a candidate structural rule. Distinguish a pointwise predictive improvement from convincing out-of-sample support given few decisions and common quarterly information. Independently reproduce coefficients/predictions using a different solver. No hypothesis test for a policy effect is implied.

Deliver observed meeting inputs, vintage register, reaction coefficients, held-out forecasts, comparative diagnostics and reproducible code. Even an accepted standalone block requires separately supplied counterfactual inflation and activity paths before use. No new GEL/USD path or persistence estimate in this step.
