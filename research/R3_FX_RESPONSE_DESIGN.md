# R3 step 2 · Georgian purchase impact and persistence

18 November 2026. Parent ea5047640a697f217184c7e4e96e21c620c040bb. The historical outcomes and previous R3 diagnostics are known. Register this specification before computing event returns or fitting response slopes. The question in this step concerns the nominal GEL-per-USD price response. Positive returns indicate lari depreciation.

1. Recover the full NBG cash-auction history from the locked workbook, from 2009 through December 2024, and the official quote series from the locked historical workbook. Keep purchases, sales, zero awards and options separate. Use calendar-day valid quotes, including carried quotes, without interpreting them as independent transactions. For a dated purchase on d, measure 100 log(E[d+h]/E[d]) at h=1, 7, 28 and 84 calendar days. Examine the preceding 7 and 28 days and a lagged 28-to-7-day placebo. The valid quote on d is the operational starting point. Timing of price-setting/announcement can vary over history. Do not infer intraday execution impact.
2. Primary auction comparison: for each purchase match one earlier nonauction date in the same year and on the same weekday, 14–91 days earlier, whose outcome horizon finishes before the purchase, with no listed auction that day. Minimize squared distances between preceding 7-day and 28-day log returns (both in log-percentage points). Break ties with the most recent date, allow reuse, show match counts and distances. Show means, matched differences and medians; split pre-2020 and 2020 onward. Show overlap with other auctions between each anchor and endpoint, and a sensitivity limited to pairs with neither window contaminated. This restriction can itself select on later market developments. Show any loss of common support. Compare purchase sizes with current monthly disclosures without treating an auction as a month of BMatch trading.
3. Monthly complementary test: extend total net market intervention to the start of the complete USD bank workbook. Reconcile its auction legs to dated auctions and overlap to existing extracts. End estimation in December 2024. Fit horizon-specific associations of 100 log(E[t+h]/E[t-1]) with positive and negative net intervention separately (magnitudes in USD 100m), at h=0, 1, 3, 6, 12, 18. Controls are an intercept, previous month's return and previous month's signed intervention. Show the preceding month's return as a selection placebo, omitting that return from controls for the placebo. Repeat for 2020–24, and only with additionally available contemporaneous broad-dollar change and VIX in 2020–24. Require full-rank designs and at least ten residual degrees of freedom. Report HAC standard errors (Bartlett, bandwidth max(1,h+1), finite-sample correction) as association uncertainty. Overlap, endogenous follow-on trades, regime changes and omitted private inflows limit interpretation. Report samples, scaling/support and the sensitivity of each association to residual confounding. For a scalar omitted factor report the equal partial R-squared with positive flow given controls and the outcome given flow/controls needed to move the slope to zero; this is a tipping point under linear omitted-variable algebra, not a bound on actual bias.
4. Use the registered external A11 nominal kernel as a quantitative challenge. In the original 57 pre-episode months compute the FX-market innovation implied by the published impact and persistence, in original units. Compare its partial correlation with current signed net intervention after residualizing both on a constant, contemporaneous and lagged policy rates, dollar change, VIX and lagged net intervention. Compare the original kernel, reversed direct effects, zero effects, and a short-lived sensitivity retaining the original impact, dropping direct lags and setting rho=0.5. These correlations measure the contemporaneous selection/shock dependence required under each specification. A correlation test based on assumed independence cannot establish exogeneity. No weighting, pooling or post-fit tuning across specifications.
5. Decision: release causal magnitude or a half-life only with independently defensible purchase variation, a verifiable first stage and comparable market/instrument support. Check the dated public schedule and exercise trigger for 2019 options as a candidate. Monthly positive net is a proxy for the treatment. Without an independently grounded bound on omitted shocks, preserve numerical descriptive and sensitivity results and withhold a structural interval. Keep the episode out of all historical fits; compare scale only. No new policy-rate model, reserve-confidence equation or episode exchange-rate simulation here.
