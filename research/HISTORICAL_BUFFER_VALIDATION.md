# Historical validation design for the reserve buffer

Frozen 22 November 2026 against Step 3 commit ff7bd7e3816a538e175ad2b3fa9647113f50958a, before calculating holdout errors. The political and economic history and Step 1 screen were already known. This is a retrospective test of rules, with an explicit publication-date audit. It cannot recover a genuinely prospective trial.

## Population and information set

Take the first quarter of each previously flagged window other than the already analyzed 2008, 2009 and 2020 windows: 2010Q1, 2012Q1, 2013Q1, 2014Q1 and 2021Q2. Add 2022Q1 as the first quarter of the remaining named window and 2018Q3 as the registered comparison. A window flagged on bank funding does not establish a sovereign FX shock. The comparison is median-ranked rather than a known crisis-free period. Keep missing cases. Use the prior calendar quarter-end as origin and evaluate one, three (primary), and twelve calendar months; do not treat nested horizons as independent episodes.

Primary target: peak cumulative eligible, same-currency public cash uses above zero initial resources and a zero floor under the Step 2 floating-rate case. A direct historical NBG reserve-draw target additionally requires the settlement route, creditor, usable Treasury cash and restrictions. Do not use changes in gross reserves as the observed gap. No cross-currency pooling, bank subsidy, discretionary market sales or contingent calls in the primary target. Do not infer bank distress or failed payments from a funding gap.

Two independently reproducible subaccounts may be evaluated when the complete national account is unobservable:

1. IMF member principal in SDR. Use the last retrievable opening (or earlier) IMF Finance projected principal schedule for that origin, including GRA repurchases and trust repayments. Prefer contractual obligations, then expectations explicitly labeled. Exclude interest, assessments and fees. Use actual member principal payments and drawings from the subsequent reconciled annual transaction tables. Give both the peak cash requirement assuming each realized drawing is usable at face value in the member account and the no-receipts outturn. An actual drawing after the cutoff is an outcome only. To earn a forecast credit before payment, a financing source must have been approved, drawable for that payer, and have a documented receipt date by the origin; otherwise give zero credit. No future approval or assumed early draw. Explicitly record the evidence supporting any credit. Missing projection rows or a provider “no data” page do not establish zero principal. Require a populated schedule extending beyond the evaluation endpoint or explicit provider coverage to identify a window with no payments.
2. The USD 6.875% Georgian sovereign bond due in April 2021, where outstanding at the origin. Use its original contractual coupon calendar and redemption amount, supported by an outstanding balance evidenced at or before the origin. Score observed debt service only with an independently dated payment confirmation or a reconciled ex-post creditor total that isolates the instrument and reporting period. Keep a contractual hindcast when the payment confirmation is incomplete. Closed instrument cohort: exclude obligations of a replacement issue. Refinancing proposals are not approved, dated cash. Respect business-day uncertainty and mark month- or quarter-only timing. Do not add the two subaccounts numerically.

Classify input evidence independently of errors: contemporaneous document demonstrably public at or before cutoff; dated as-of reconstruction with first-publication time unavailable; later/revised; missing. Retrieve the original official records first. Calendar-end web query dates alone cannot certify when that schedule was available. Record whether outcomes are day-dated, interval-censored, or unavailable. An unavailable national outcome remains unscored even if a component matches exactly.

## Prediction, comparisons and scoring

For each available account, post scheduled eligible uses on their contractual dates. Subtract only eligible, dated pre-approved funding on its permitted date. The buffer is the maximum cumulative deficit, including the opening zero. A positive peak date is the first month reaching the maximum. Report peak timing only when both peaks are positive and identifiable. The simple debt-service comparator holds the full sum of scheduled principal (or coupon plus redemption for the bond), ignores new financing, and has the same zero initial nonreserve resources, currency, horizon and account boundary. For timing, use the first month when its cumulative gross bill reaches the recommended stock. If neither model admits funding and all bills are nonnegative the two forecasts coincide by construction. Preserve ties.

Report predicted amount, realized amount or identified interval, nonnegative uncovered funding, excess opening stock and signed/absolute peak-month error. For an outturn interval [L,U], uncovered amounts range from max(0,L−B) to max(0,U−B); excess ranges from max(0,B−U) to max(0,B−L). Treat blanks as missing. Disclose gross emergency/drawing amounts separately with their source, and whether they were newly approved, pre-existing or of unresolved commitment status. A missed unit means a unit of unfinanced payment under the account assumptions. Calculate excess-buffer cost at **100 basis points per year pro rata over the horizon** as a scenario in the same currency, with zero cost as a comparison. Do not claim a historical marginal yield spread.

Record IMF ARA stocks or coverage published before cutoff when available. A national ARA reserve stock covers a wider objective than one creditor or bond. Only score it formally when the stock, coverage perimeter, currency and horizon are comparable. No pro-rata allocation of ARA to the tested creditors. Rank on the paired three-month results: first total uncovered within currency and account, then lower excess given equal uncovered. Never pool unlike accounts or forecast grades. Repeat excluding each episode and explicitly flag prior development cases as excluded, including 2008. No-fitting rule: use every eligible selected origin, no calibration on holdouts. Claim a demonstrated improvement on the primary test only with comparable contemporary inputs and outturns, at least one stress and one comparison episode, no worse uncovered amount in each, strictly lower excess in at least one, and no reversal when one episode is omitted. Otherwise report exactly what was validated and restrict the next stage to conditional stress applications.
