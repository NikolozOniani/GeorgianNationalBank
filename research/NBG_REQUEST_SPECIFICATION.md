# Specification for records not obtainable from public sources

This specification implements the fixed [research protocol](PROTOCOL.md). It is **not a sent request** and makes no claim that the requested records are confidential or unpublished. First complete the public checks in [the acquisition plan](DATA_REQUIREMENTS.md).

Research purpose: evaluate the 2025–2026 purchase episode and the economic consequences of the same episode without discretionary market purchases. Request **1 January 2025 through the latest executed period**, an opening balance at **31 December 2024**, and later updates through **31 December 2026** once available. Seek comparable 2020–2024 history where it is readily available, without delaying the episode records.

Use NBG's [public-information channel](https://nbg.gov.ge/en/about-us/public-information) after approval to send correspondence. Ask for existing CSV/XLSX extracts and definitions, not a new bespoke causal analysis. Preserve response date, covered period, revision status, legal restrictions and any denied fields.

| Package | Requested fields | Confidentiality-preserving fallback | Analytical purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1: executed market FX operations | Instrument, currency, direction, gross amount, execution price/GEL countervalue, trade timestamp/timezone, value date, cancellation/revision and disclosure date; confirm any options/swaps/forwards | No bank names or identifiers; daily gross totals by instrument/currency with volume-weighted price; if necessary, delayed/weekly aggregates with limitations stated | Define treatment; separate execution, settlement and later disclosure; reconcile public monthly/annual totals |
| R2: intervention rule and decision information | Rule versions/effective dates, thresholds/caps, information available before order placement, timing conventions, discretionary exceptions and changes | Retrospective methodology, coarse trigger bins, summary exception flags, safe-room research access if available | Test selection, anticipation and potential identifying variation; public option rules must not be presumed to govern BMatch |
| R3: government transactions and reserve bridge | Market versus government FX transactions; borrowing/grant receipts, conversions and debt service; reserve income; FX/gold/securities valuation; SDR/IMF changes; reclassifications and residuals | Monthly category totals with original currency and conversion convention; no counterparty identity | Reconcile reserve change and separate market demand from nonmarket financing/valuation |
| R4: monetary-liquidity counterpart and costs | Daily/monthly average bank settlement balances, refinancing and other operation stocks, CDs/facilities, remuneration rates, maturities, accrued costs and interest income; government deposits/cash demand | Aggregated currency/instrument buckets; no commercial-bank allocation or collateral identity | Distinguish additional absorption from reduced refinancing and match incremental funding cost |
| R5: reserve return and usability | Monthly/quarterly average assets by currency/instrument/maturity; accrued interest and fees; price/FX gains; encumbrances; predetermined/contingent drains; derivative exposures | Broad currency/duration buckets and published risk limits; no security-by-security positions or trading strategy | Estimate matched return/exposure, available reserve liquidity and stress coverage |
| R6: ARA reconciliation | Exact realized component vectors, vintage/date, national series mapping, exchange-rate conversion, short-term debt residual-maturity calculation and adjusted-bank-buffer formula | Methodology and aggregate inputs sufficient to reproduce published ratios | Resolve denominator differences and the historical bank-adjustment residual without inferring error from a definition gap |

For every package request the unit, valuation convention, gross/net definition, stock/flow basis, reporting population, calendar/timezone, missing/zero codes, publication lag, revision policy and confidentiality classification. If a field cannot be disclosed, request the specific restriction and the maximum safe aggregation. Failure to provide a field narrows the empirical claims; it does not establish misconduct.

Separate possible contacts, not included in an NBG request: MoF/Treasury for government cash/FX settlement and issue terms; IMF/authors for model code/calibration; market-data providers for licensed quote histories. No person-directed message or application has been sent as part of repository organization.
