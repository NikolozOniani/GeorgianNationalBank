**Prepared 10 November 2026; not sent.** This supplements the [existing request specification](NBG_REQUEST_SPECIFICATION.md), not an authorization to contact NBG. Request existing records for **1 January 2025–30 September 2026**, with **31 December 2024** opening stocks. Later months may be supplied as a separately labeled vintage; they do not silently extend the registered episode.

If a field is restricted, request the legal basis and the maximum safe aggregation; a nonresponse or refusal is not evidence of policy harm. These records concern measurement first. They do not automatically establish exogenous intervention variation.
