# Historical FX funding shocks: step 1 screening specification

19 November 2026. Freeze before tabulating ranks. This implements section 7 of the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md). Historical crises are known. This is a retrospective comparison using revised observations, not a forecast evaluation.

Use calendar quarters from 2008Q1 through 2024Q4. Mark the five highest strictly positive pressure scores per channel (ties at the cutoff included). Keep all scores and ranks, including negative pressure. Group adjacent flagged quarters into one catalogue window across channels. Preserve 2008, 2014–15, 2020 and 2022 as named review windows even when unflagged. Retain as a low-pressure comparison the common-data quarter closest to the median rank on all three channels (minimum sum of absolute deviations, earlier quarter breaks ties). This comparison need not be crisis-free. No probability interpretation or composite severity ranking.

- External receipts: year-on-year decline in quarterly goods and services export credits divided by the prior-year quarter's credits, in USD. Accrual-based proxy for export receipts, excluding remittances and capital inflows.
- Bank external loan funding: negative quarterly net incurrence of deposit-taking corporations' external loan liabilities divided by the prior quarter-end stock of their short- and long-term external loans. Measures net loan repayment pressure. Gross debt due and actual refinancing refusal remain unknown.
- Resident FX deposit pressure: decline in quarter-end broad-money FX deposits, expressed in USD at official quarter-end GEL/USD, divided by opening USD-equivalent deposits. This stock proxy includes non-USD cross-rates, interest and composition changes. Preserve monthly levels and short-lived changes in the 2008 reconstruction. Missing opening quotes stay missing.

A reserve sale, reserve-stock decline or emergency disbursement does not select an episode. Use NBG source-manifest vintages and original units. Use actual quarter-end dates and preserve annual columns separately from quarterly flows. Publish levels, denominators and gaps. Do not impute absent observations. For 2008, report month-end reserve changes separately from the IMF's within-month war chronology; retain the unallocated residual and quarterly transaction/stock bridge. No estimate of unavoidable public dollar needs until payers, overlap and due dates are verified.
