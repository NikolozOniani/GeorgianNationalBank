# What the Georgian reserve buffer must cover

**Decision, 20 November 2026.** For the primary case, size the buffer for binding public foreign-currency payments under a floating lari. Price bank support and market sales as two separate policy choices. This freezes the rules before the minimum-buffer calculation. Historical outcomes were known when the rules were chosen. [Research roadmap, section 7](../../research/ROADMAP.md#7-reserve-adequacy-from-georgian-funding-shocks)

## Eligible payments and the boundary of each account

| Case | Amount admitted at each settlement date | Evidence needed to charge the NBG account |
|---|---|---|
| Public payments under a float | Binding NBG commitments plus uncovered public FX bills under the assumed public backstop. No discretionary sales. | Named payer, currency, due date or maturity interval, amount, and usable funds by that date. Report the assumed Treasury backstop separately from legally binding NBG obligations. |
| Public payments plus emergency bank support | Add the remaining eligible bank payment after the bank uses its available FX. The scenario offers up to 100% of this residual for solvent banks with eligible collateral, capped at the collateral’s realizable value. | For each eligible bank, verified withdrawals or maturing external bills, failed or unavailable replacement funding, own accessible FX, collateral and delivery currency. The aggregate banking system cannot transfer one bank's assets to another by assumption. |
| Public payments plus market stabilization | Add the declared net-sales budget at its observed frequency. Base budget is zero. For the historical sales replay, use $187m in August 2008, $142m in 2009Q1, and $24.80m, $40.50m and $55.65m in April, May and June 2020. | Treat each replay amount as a chosen budget. Quarter-end net sales leave intraperiod gross execution and sequencing unknown. The 2008 temporary peg was a policy choice. Its sales cannot identify the amount needed to maintain a particular exchange rate during 2025–26. |

The [payment-by-payment matrix](policy_matrix.csv) assigns each cash use to a single account. Bank support and sales are separate additions to the public case. A joint stress would require evidence that the operations are disjoint, particularly if a bank bought FX at an auction to make a payment. A called guarantee enters public payments once, on the government's evidenced call or settlement. Guarantee stocks, unrealized deposit withdrawals, stock declines, and falls in exports do not enter the primary ledger.

**Choose one source of obligations for each payer and instrument.** In a dated-payment replay, use the IMF repayments and the bond coupon once. Preserve projected IMF charges in a separate schedule scenario. A quarterly fiscal envelope covers a wider set of flows and replaces its overlapping dated payments. In an opening-date forecast use section II.1 of the reserve template as a separate aggregate account. Its 1-, 3- and 12-month columns are cumulative. Section III.1 contains contingencies; admit a call once, only with an explicit call assumption and after checking overlap with bank support. Table totals, component rows, actual settlements and scheduled drains are alternative descriptions of some of the same uses. Where crosswalks fail, run separate cases. [Horizon-specific calls, USD million with outflows negative](scheduled_calls.csv) · [IMF template definitions](https://www.imf.org/external/np/sta/ir/IRProcessWeb/pdf/chapter1.pdf)

**Keep two public balances.** First pay each Treasury bill from its unrestricted same-currency resources. An external Treasury account can make the payment directly. A Treasury FX deposit *at NBG* is different: drawing on it can settle the fiscal bill in full while NBG still delivers reserves to the creditor. In the gross reserve ledger count that delivery once. If the deposit has already been excluded from freely available opening reserves, debit its segregated balance when used. Never both deduct it from opening stock and charge the same disbursement to the free balance. For the $17.1875m April 2020 coupon, full payment from an external Treasury account implies zero NBG delivery. Full payment from a Treasury FX deposit at NBG implies the same zero fiscal shortfall but $17.1875m of gross NBG delivery. With no Treasury FX and an assumed full backstop, both the funding gap and NBG delivery equal $17.1875m. The paying account remains unknown. [Three reproducible settlement cases, USD](coupon_routes.csv)

Apply the same logic to bank reserve deposits and collateral. Transferring existing public FX between two domestic accounts does not create external resources.

For undocumented Treasury cash, run an adverse timing case with no verified offset before payment, and a favorable **conditional** case allowing external funds to cover a bill up to its face amount. Keep the Treasury-at-NBG payment as a separate settlement route. Unavailable data prevent assigning probabilities or equating favorable fiscal coverage with zero external reserve use. A quarter-only payment can be placed first or last within its interval to test timing, respecting any known events. Same-day ordering remains unresolved unless value times are known.

Their sum is $0.000351m below the FX component in the monthly reserve stock series. The reconciliation retains that source difference and uses the directly reported components in the discounted case. July 2008 falls between IIP quarter-ends; the earlier June portfolio is not substituted.

At the purchase episode opening the NBG reported $1,279.08m in FX currency and deposits, $2,118.06m in FX securities and a negative $1.17m in other reserve assets. Cash plus securities at par leaves out $598.73m gold, $453.83m SDRs and $0.03m IMF reserve position. A 5% discount on securities alone reduces that conditional amount by $105.90m. These are measures of asset access **before payments**. The baseline admits all components at reported values only under immediate, frictionless, currency-matched mobilization. No verified free-cash total can be established from the reporting template for these dates.

The delayed-sale stress assumes deposits accessible at opening and securities proceeds after two business days, with 5% deducted. The delay and discount are sensitivity choices. Actual settlement calendars, currencies, haircuts and custody restrictions need transaction evidence. For an obligation falling between available dates, withhold the proceeds until settlement or bracket the timing if the applicable business calendar is unknown. Positive miscellaneous assets, gold, SDRs and the IMF reserve position have zero access in the narrow FX cases. Negative miscellaneous positions are carried at face value. A currency-specific replay may admit SDR holdings against SDR payments once transferability is established. Do not simultaneously use those SDRs as USD funding. Missing pledge and repo memo amounts prevent a verified encumbrance deduction. Aggregate currency composition in USD equivalent cannot establish USD cash in hand. [Resource eligibility and settlement rules](asset_rules.csv) · [R2 source schedule](../r2_2026_11_11/liquidity_schedule.csv)

**Operating floor.** Use zero for bare payment coverage. As an independent sensitivity, keep 1% of observed opening gross reserves unspent at every date, fixed at the observed origin even when testing smaller opening stocks. For December 2024 that is $44.49m. This scale choice measures how much requiring cash left over changes the result. It carries no claim about an optimal or legally prescribed floor. The historical 2009 program floor applies to net international reserves at program exchange rates and only at the test date. A gross-dollar cash floor cannot substitute for that test.

## Funding that arrives after the shock

[Funding cases](funding_cases.csv) give each facility's beneficiary, original currency, approval and actual receipt date where known. The actual-arrival case admits at most the documented gross receipt when it arrives. Deduct fees and apply restrictions before making it available to a payer. For month-only arrivals, report earliest/latest admissible within-month placement. The **no-new-rescue** case retains own external assets and facilities already approved or signed at the opening, on their actual draw dates and subject to their conditions. It excludes tranches requiring fresh discretionary review approval. This strict rule can exclude previously *scheduled* conditional access. A pre-existing arrangement alone confers no spendable opening cash. The World Bank operation was signed before the March quarter-end, after the pandemic had begun; this classification uses the quarter opening.

| Window | Realized funding | No-new-rescue treatment |
|---|---|---|
| August–September 2008 | First IMF drawing about $250m in September. Treasury repatriated about $50m of its own foreign assets. | Exclude the new IMF receipt. Allow the Treasury transfer on actual arrival only if the external asset was not already credited at opening. |
| 2009Q1 | Combined SDR 126.2m drawn 25 March. | Retain SDR 63.1m on 25 March from the completed December review. Exclude the second SDR 63.1m. Earlier drawability of the first tranche is a separate timing sensitivity. |
| 2020Q2 | IMF SDR 147m on 5 May. Original World Bank EUR 45m operation disbursed on 6 May. | Exclude the IMF tranche requiring the May review. Retain the World Bank facility on its May draw date, subject to net proceeds and permitted use. ADB CARES had zero Q2 cash under both cases and disbursed on 8 July. |

The March 2020 twelve-month window also includes the SDR 79m IMF drawing of 18 December. The strict no-new-rescue case excludes it. The pre-pandemic program had scheduled future reviews, each subject to approval. Excluding a tranche is an assumption about new approvals and does not classify every dollar of the tranche as an augmentation. [December 2019 review, table 9](https://www.imf.org/-/media/Files/Publications/CR/2019/1GEOEA2019003.ashx) · [December 2020 review decision](https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2020/12/16/pr20377-georgia-imf-executive-board-completes-seventh-review-under-eff)

A split facility replaces the single IMF transaction for the same drawing. The World Bank and IMF drawings sit inside broader MoF creditor aggregates. The remaining gross drawings have incomplete timing and restrictions. Leave the unidentified portion unresolved; adding the quarterly total would duplicate known receipts. Relevant dates and source evidence are in the [historical payment calendar](../historical_fx_shocks/payment_calendar/README.md). These exclusions hold other events fixed for accounting and do not predict what Georgia would have done without assistance.

## Evidence for bank self-insurance

At end-March 2020 banks held $790.59m in short-term external deposit assets and reported zero short-term external debt-security assets. Short-term external deposit liabilities were $1,083.83m. Neither the asset balance nor the difference establishes a call on NBG. A deposit can be unavailable to the bank, in the wrong currency, or already needed for another liability. The $65m private repayment in January 2009 likewise supplies a debtor and amount, with no evidence of a public cash payment. [Bank positions and ownership-specific access sensitivities](bank_assets.csv)

Freeze access fractions of 0%, 50% and 100% of the bank’s own eligible external deposits and securities. For each eligible bank at each date, deduct usable own resources and available replacement funding from eligible payments once, floor the remaining need at zero, and cap the loan at realizable collateral. Reduce remaining resources when used. A sector aggregate cannot verify bank-specific coverage, collateral, solvency, currency matching or note delivery. The June 2008 IIP observation predates the August shock by a month-end; carry its observation date. For December 2008 and March 2020 the positions align with the quarter opening. ## Handoff to the buffer calculation

Step 3 can compute restricted public-payment paths, alternative aggregate schedule paths, and historical sales-budget scenarios separately. It can calculate the effect of an assumed haircut, payment-order change or funding delay. A comprehensive compulsory public buffer remains unidentified wherever opening Treasury FX by account location, gross payment dates, currency-specific settlement, or lien releases are missing. The bank backstop remains conditional wherever individual bank resources and collateral cannot be matched. Public-payment coverage, support for banks and sales should therefore produce separate conclusions.
