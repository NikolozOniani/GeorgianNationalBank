# Actionable reform agenda

These are proposals, not assertions of existing duties or proven violations. Current legal baseline: LAW Articles 20, 60–61 and CONSTITUTION Article 68 in [sources.md](sources.md). NBG already publishes financial/policy material. The aim is better usability, reconciliation and evaluation while protecting policy independence. No proposal requires advance public announcement of active trading triggers.

| Priority / implementer | Concrete deliverable and acceptance test | Household/accountability value | Tradeoff and safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. NBG statistics/market operations | One bulk historical FX table, separately auctions and BMatch: gross purchases, gross sales, net, currency, units, execution/settlement period, revision status, publication timestamp; downloadable CSV/API. Monthly sums reconcile to annual reports or explain differences. Publish a single binding release calendar; explain old 25-day versus third-week wording. | Makes scale and timing checkable; prevents treating reserves as intervention | Daily BMatch patterns can expose triggers. Retain appropriate aggregate delay, justify it publicly, and review whether longer-lag historical detail is safe. A proposed deadline is not a claim of current noncompliance. |
| 2. NBG finance with Ministry of Finance | Monthly reserve bridge: opening/closing stock; NBG intervention; government FX; other transactions; investment income treatment; currency/gold valuation; other volume changes; sign, perimeter and settlement metadata. Residual explicitly reported, never forced to zero. | Separates asset movements from expenses; makes stock-flow questions answerable | Different accounting systems/timing create genuine differences. Reconcile staged provisional/final vintages; suppress sensitive small cells where justified. |
| 3. NBG finance/operations and external financial auditors | Instrument-level liquidity/sterilization table: outstanding and average balances, maturity, currency, interest paid/accrued, fees and collateral rules. Explain links to audited income and profit transfers. | Tests carry and subsidy claims without crude reserve×policy-rate calculations | Commercial sensitivity, currency risk and insurance value defeat naive rankings. Publish aggregates, accrual basis and risk-adjusted alternatives; specialist access to restricted detail under law. |
| 4. NBG research/supervision with Geostat | Annual distributional annex: borrower/depositor/currency exposures by anonymized income/expenditure groups; separate price, debt-service, employment and remittance channels; baseline and alternative assumptions; validated category crosswalk, survey design and unmatched shares | Identifies potentially concentrated burdens and benefits | Old surveys and missing balance sheets can mislead. Date every input, report coverage and sampling limitations, avoid a false single welfare number. Geostat statistical independence and confidentiality preserved. |
| 5. Parliament's relevant committee | Structured annual hearing on objectives, forecasts versus outcomes, alternatives considered, reserve use, uncertainty and distribution; written answers and follow-up register. Invite technically qualified opposing views. | Turns an annual report into contestable reasoning | Politicization can undermine stability. No parliamentary instruction on an individual rate or trade; no compulsory disclosure of live triggers. Financial audits and policy evaluation are different functions. |
| 6. NBG Board / qualified independent reviewers | Periodic ex-post methodological review with public terms, conflicts declarations, reproducible non-sensitive models/data, secure access where legally allowed, public response to recommendations | Allows adverse and favorable results to be tested | Access rights cannot be invented. Constitution 68 limits State Audit Service scrutiny to administrative/capital expenditures; do not present unrestricted monetary-policy audit by that service as current law. Use lawful specialist review or seek carefully scoped legislation where necessary. |
| 7. NBG information governance | Non-sensitive register of withheld data categories, legal basis, restriction/review period, responsible office and available aggregates; reasoned response to requests; machine-readable revision archive | Distinguishes confidentiality, delay and retrieval failure | A detailed inventory can itself reveal strategies. Publish categories and legal reasoning, not active rules or personal account data; legal review and aggregate alternatives. |
| 8. Government/Parliament, informed by NBG analysis | Evaluate targeted fiscal relief for demonstrably exposed households against inflationary and budget costs; publish eligibility, funding and sunset conditions | Compensation addresses incidence without forcing NBG to abandon price stability | Universal cheap credit or unfinanced transfers can worsen inflation and moral hazard. Relief is not justified by our hypothetical examples alone; require actual exposure and fiscal assessment. |

## Proposed implementation sequence

Within a **proposed 90-day** initial work plan: publish locations of existing files; resolve definitions and release calendar; list remaining requests and legal restrictions. Within a **proposed six-month** pilot: deliver FX table, provisional reserve bridge and reproducible distributional scenario template. These are proposed targets, not statutory deadlines or evidence that implementation takes exactly that long. An independent review should report incomplete fields as well as completed milestones.

Success metrics: downloadable coverage by month/instrument; proportion of flows reconciled under declared tolerance; number and age of unexplained residuals; actual versus announced publication lag; revision traceability; household-exposure coverage and unmatched shares. Set numerical tolerances after measuring data precision. Do not reward artificially zero residuals or premature disclosure.

## Specific records to request

1. For every month January 2020–December 2024: executed gross FX purchases/sales through each instrument, dates where publicly releasable, currency and unit, settlement convention, first publication and revision dates. Ask for existing links before new extraction.
2. The same-window reserve reconciliation and government FX entries, with amounts classified independently from NBG intervention. Ask how accrued income and revaluation enter the bridge.
3. Sterilization/refinancing balances and accrued costs by instrument, currency and maturity; annual audited reconciliation; aggregate counterparty concentration and collateral haircuts where safe.
4. Anonymized loan/deposit exposures by currency, interest type and reset date; household survey design/crosswalk information needed to assess incidence. No customer identities requested.
5. Current legal basis and review date for restrictions; a safe alternative aggregation where exact fields cannot be released. Ask which historical records are genuinely not maintained.

No requests have been sent. A refusal, if later received, should be quoted accurately, assessed against its legal basis and distinguished from failure to find a public link. Reform advocacy does not require accusing NBG of bad faith.
