## Ready responses under hostile questioning

- **“So have you proved the National Bank made people poorer?”** No net causal household effect is established. We documented prices and operations, explained conditional burdens and benefits, and specified the records needed to evaluate choices. Saying more would be false.
- **“The IMF says intervention helped.”** The speech includes that finding. Its model is relevant counterevidence; it neither supplies every household's welfare effect nor removes the duty to disclose usable accounts.
- **“You cannot even map the household basket.”** We report the exact unmatched share. That is why we do not publish a total household inflation or poverty effect. Hiding that gap would undermine the investigation.
- **“You called an accounting difference missing money.”** No. The residual is explicitly an unallocated composite, not a loss. Do not use that accusation in headlines or interviews.
- **“The information is already public.”** Supply the series URL and metadata; we will verify, incorporate it and correct our gap classification. An unsuccessful download is not evidence of concealment.
- **“Would you order the bank to cut rates?”** This evidence does not justify a rate prescription. It justifies explanation of alternatives, exposure reporting and retrospective scrutiny while preserving operational independence.
- **“Who is corrupt or colluding?”** This investigation establishes no corruption, illegal conduct, collusion or intent to impoverish. It names none.

## Publication discipline

Preserve qualifications in subtitles, slides and short clips. Never combine the partial 2017 contribution with the national 2020–2024 CPI as if they were one estimand. Do not imply that annual independent survey samples track the same households. Do not announce today's rate using the historical panel. No live transaction rule or personal account record is requested for public release. A revised legal or empirical source must trigger a documented correction, not silent replacement of the evidence.
