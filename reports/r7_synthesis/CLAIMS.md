## Strong counterarguments

**Fundamental inflows and endogenous purchases.** A central bank that buys when foreign earnings or private inflows are strong will be observed buying into appreciation even if its trades have little effect. Historical Georgian purchase auctions already showed pretrends. This interpretation is compatible with the actual lari gaining 7.74%. The zero-impact case survives as an empirical challenger. The registered conditional paths quantify what follows if private portfolios demand a price concession. [Georgian FX diagnostics](../r3_fx_response/README.md)

**Preventing temporary overshooting can benefit the economy.** A one-off rise above fundamentals could hurt exporters and destabilize currency-mismatched borrowers when it reverses. The IMF's 2025 exercise finds short-run benefits possible in a shallow market facing risk-appetite shocks. A three-month direct half-life in the present model nearly halves the central currency gain. The 2025–26 data do not identify the fundamental exchange rate, the source of each inflow or an avoided disorderly reversal. The separate Georgian monetary-shock diagnostic also implies weaker policy transmission than the IMF estimates. A tighter historical fit for the CPI target alone would not settle these alternatives. [IMF 2025, paragraphs 23–26](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2025/english/1geoea2025002-source-pdf.pdf) · [Monetary-shock comparison](../r3_policy_feedback/README.md)

**The insurance could easily be worth more than the carry.** It could. Costs fall sharply with low-interest funding. If a crisis destroys 20% of the GDP benchmark, the real-resource test needs only a 1.31 pp risk reduction at a 1% opportunity spread. If the same loss is 5%, it needs 4.86 pp. Liquidity could lower spreads, reduce forced sales or protect bank solvency even when projected public dues fit. These channels must be priced with disjoint losses and eligible access. Assuming they are worthless would be as unwarranted as pricing every deliverable reserve dollar at a dollar of national benefit. [Severity sensitivity](resource_frontier.csv)

**The state could have borrowed instead.** The observed sovereign issue supports access at one date. Its five-year yield cannot be assumed available, at any volume, on the date of a crisis or for emergency central-bank liquidity. Private bank FX is usable by its owner only subject to currency matching, solvency and collateral. The necessary replacement cost per dollar and the matched external-funding scenario make this objection testable without inventing a lender. [Funding scenarios](reserve_stresses.csv) · [Joint thresholds](joint_results.csv)

## Accounts that must remain separate

| Item | Correct treatment in the synthesis |
|---|---|
| USD principal, interest, FX marks | Retain the asset and its funding obligation. Use either accrued interest or the marked liquidation account with retained interest. |
| Domestic bank interest, Treasury interest and dividends | Private recipients retain income. Eliminate matching NBG–Treasury transfers in the consolidated account. A counterparty's residence and actual alternative use matter for national resources. |
| Sovereign issuance | Value the incremental issue proceeds once. They can reflect the same crisis mitigation priced in a hazard model. |
| Public, bank and market claims | Add market sales or eligible bank residuals only when they represent disjoint uses. A Treasury deposit at NBG is a claim on the same reserves. Cumulative maturity horizons and rolling origin tests overlap. |
| Cash protection, replacement expense and crisis loss | Shortfall avoided is a stock service. Replacement-cost and crisis-loss tests are different valuations of that service and can overlap. |
| Household baskets, national CPI, exporters and dollar receipts | Report channel-specific incidence with its sign and unit. Overlapping households and purchaser/recipient roles prevent summing group gains into social welfare. |
| Ending asset and insurance service | Assign the terminal asset and liability consistently. Report the assumed insurance residual and the additional present value needed to reverse the ranking. |
