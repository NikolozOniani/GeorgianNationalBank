## Findings requiring repair

### A1 — Medium: source verification is not bound to the inputs used in calculation

In the audited `src/r6_benefits.py`, lines 94–121 verify hard-coded values in the PDFs. `generate()` separately reads `research/r6_bond_terms.json`. The verifier does not compare those JSON values directly with extracted source values.

**Demonstrated failure:** changing `face_usdm` from 500 to 5,000 in memory passes both `verify_sources()` and `generate()`, changing the financing table despite the source still saying USD 500 million. The in-memory test wrote no altered observations or outputs. Exact `--check` would detect departure from the existing output snapshot; it would not validate an incorrectly regenerated and re-pinned snapshot against the source.

**Required repair:** extract the relevant values from the pinned PDFs and compare them to the calculation inputs, including face value, coupon, yield, price, benchmark, spread and dates. Validate both original issues. Add tests that corrupt the calculation input while leaving the PDF unchanged and require rejection before generation. Hard-coded source fragments can supplement this binding but cannot substitute for it.

**Effect on current findings:** none found. The audit's separate parser verifies that the committed values are correct.

### A2 — Medium: payment-frequency metadata is silently ignored

The JSON declares `payments_per_year`, but production pricing calls rely on the function's default of two payments per year, and the discount conversion is hard-coded to divide by two. The provided `years` field is not directly checked against settlement and maturity dates by production source validation.

**Demonstrated failure:** changing `payments_per_year` from 2 to 1 passes source verification and generation and leaves every numerical CSV unchanged. The declared configuration and calculation can therefore disagree silently.

**Required repair:** either enforce the supported five-year, semiannual contract and reject incompatible metadata, or explicitly pass the verified payment frequency through all pricing, discounting and inverse calculations. Validate settlement/maturity against the tenor. Test that a frequency mismatch cannot pass silently.

**Effect on current findings:** none found. The real bond is semiannual, five years; those are the conventions the calculation currently uses.

These are verification defects, not discoveries that the present bond arithmetic or source observations are false. The audit intentionally leaves production code unchanged so the reviewed commit and failure demonstrations remain reviewable.

## Limits on the research conclusion

- **The registered counterfactual remains absent.** The USD 100m addition above observed reserves and static target increments are sensitivity experiments. They do not estimate the path without the episode's purchases or prevented lari appreciation.
- **The financial hurdle is not national welfare.** The arbitrary 0/0.5/1 resource-cost-share grid has no empirical calibration and is not a confidence interval or bound on social costs. Domestic interest transfers, ownership, taxes and alternative uses require separate economic accounting.
- **One bond is not the entire financing benefit.** The result cannot refute all IMF insurance channels or benefits to other borrowers. Its assumed increment would need to precede pricing; purchases after issuance cannot retroactively lower its original terms.
- **Insurance remains unestimated.** Joint stress probabilities, crisis loss duration, currency-specific access, overlapping calls and alternative financing are not identified. Historical net FX sales do not supply that distribution.
- **The IMF recommendation remains incompletely reproduced.** Two printed channels and target translations do not establish the full objective's optimum. Existing criticisms must remain conditional on the printed specification and missing implementation details.

**Stage decision:** retain the original R6 results as conditional diagnostics, retain G4 as partial, repair A1/A2 before revised-source reuse, then proceed to R7's common-account and claim audit. R6 strengthens what can be tested; it does not yet establish that the purchases were harmful, beneficial or excessive.
