# Proposed evidence request — draft, not sent

Purpose: measure contractual household debt-service exposure during 2020–2024 without confusing it with a causal welfare estimate. Ask NBG first whether the records or tabulations already exist and which public source contains them. A failure to locate a table in this study is not proof that it is unpublished or unlawfully withheld.

## Minimum public aggregate release

Quarterly or monthly joint tabulations, with stable definitions, of household loan balances and borrower counts by residency, currency, purpose, fixed/floating/hybrid type, contractual benchmark, reset interval and remaining maturity band. Report entrepreneur status separately where feasible. Add broad verified-income and debt-service bands if disclosure risk permits. Provide weighted distributions rather than only an average rate. Publish cell suppression rules, revisions, original release dates and break flags.

Also distinguish outstanding from new/renegotiated loans; scheduled from paid interest; performing from arrears/defaulted loans; and market-rate from subsidized contracts. Identify treatment of zero-interest contracts, payment holidays, accrued unpaid interest, fees and capitalized interest. Avoid cross-tab dimensions so fine that borrowers or commercially sensitive bank positions become identifiable.

## Restricted research access, if lawful

Pseudonymous linked loan-month records, spanning a pre-2020 baseline through 2024, with opening/closing principal, contractual and effective rates, benchmark and margin, reset dates, remaining term, scheduled and actual payments, fees, arrears, default, collateral/purpose, refinancing/prepayment, and subsidy flags. Record borrower income definition, measurement date, income changes, other debt and deposits only to the extent lawful and necessary.

Secure access and output checking are preferable to public release of individual records. No names, personal identification numbers, addresses or counterparty identities are needed. Consent, legal basis, retention limits and disclosure review must be resolved by the data custodian and research institution before any transfer.

## Questions these records would answer

| Record | Estimand unlocked | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Currency × benchmark × reset × balance | Weighted contractual exposure to declared rate paths | Still conditional on a reference path; not net macro welfare |
| Actual bills, arrears and refinancing | Difference between scheduled and realized cash flow | Behavioral selection and default must be modeled or described |
| Linked verified income and deposits | Distribution of debt-service pressure and offsetting interest income | Incomplete assets, informal income and consumption remain relevant |
| Predetermined reset dates and contract histories | Candidate differential-exposure design | Endogenous contract choice, anticipation and refinancing can invalidate causality |
| Historical vintages and first-release timestamps | Information set available at each policy decision | Does not itself identify an exogenous policy shock |

The requested response should distinguish: already public; available with delay; confidential but releasable in aggregates; restricted research access possible; not collected; and unavailable for another stated reason. Do not demand public counterparty disclosure as a substitute for an appropriately protected household exposure dataset.
