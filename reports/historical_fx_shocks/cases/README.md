# The 2009 funding squeeze and the 2020 export shock

Step 1 case reconstruction, 19 November 2026. Six quarters and 18 months from the [registered historical screen](../../../research/HISTORICAL_FX_SHOCK_SCREEN.md). The primary questions here are who owed FX, when official funds arrived, and how observed reserve transactions differed from the external trade shock. These ledgers use historical outturns. They do not simulate alternative reserve stocks.

## 2009: private debt came due while imports contracted

In 2009Q1 banks repaid USD 116.03m in external loan liabilities on net (short-term net repayment USD 134.45m, long-term net borrowing USD 18.42m). In Q2 the net repayment was almost identical, USD 116.41m, but USD 95.49m came from the long-term leg. A nearly unchanged total concealed a change in the maturity of the liabilities being repaid. Net incurrence does not reveal what portion of gross maturities lenders declined to refinance.

A named private payment is recoverable. Bank of Georgia's 19 August prospectus, physical PDF p113, records USD 91m **face value** repurchased in the first half and USD 49m repaid in full in June after holders exercised a put. The latter is a dated private USD use. The USD 140m face amount spans multiple transactions; the price paid for the USD 91m is unknown. The bank also reports drawing its USD 200m IFC/EBRD package and redeeming USD 260.1m of wholesale funding by the prospectus date. The facility was agreed in December 2008. These later cumulative amounts overlap the notes and run beyond June. No evidence here assigns the bank's repayment to NBG or matches a specific facility dollar to a particular redemption. [Borrower and policy ledger with physical PDF pages](document_ledger.csv) · [Original PDF URLs and hashes](pdf_sources.json)

The IMF records four principal payments totaling SDR 7.38m before the SDR 126.2m stand-by drawing on 25 March, and SDR 0.90m paid on 1 May. The Q1 USD balance-of-payments (BOP) records USD 179.98m net IMF credit, allocated to the central bank. The Q2 corresponding entry is minus USD 1.24m. IMF cash dates establish order and native-currency amounts; the BOP values are net quarterly observations with a different accounting basis. An announced USD equivalent is insufficient to convert every SDR payment. [Eight dated IMF transactions across both cases](imf_cash.csv)

Gross reserves fell USD 210.83m over January and February combined and rose USD 223.78m in March. The resulting USD 12.95m Q1 stock increase obscures the earlier liquidity drawdown. In Q2 exports fell USD 312.49m against a year earlier but imports fell USD 856.83m, improving the goods-and-services balance by USD 544.33m. That trade offset helps explain why the export decline alone is a poor measure of reserve need. Imports can fall through economically costly adjustment.

The contemporaneous authorities' statement reports all-market net sales of USD 142m in Q1. NBG's Q2 report gives USD 0.4m of net supply across the interbank exchange and auctions. The dated auction workbook alone yields USD 14m and USD 51.7m of net sales, respectively. Q1 includes intervention before auctions began. Both venues operated until 25 May. Q2 contains a USD 51.3m difference between the two coverage concepts. Keep it as an unallocated cross-source difference: the USD 95.4m auction supply mentioned in the Q2 narrative also does not reconcile to the workbook's Q2 rows. The same report identifies USD 32.2m of intragovernmental conversions separately. No records here allocate those conversions to debt-service dates. [Monthly auction sums and reserve stocks](monthly.csv) · [NBG venue change](https://nbg.gov.ge/en/media/news/national-bank-of-georgia-shall-perform-interventions-through-currency-auctions)

## 2020: the export loss exceeded the deterioration in the trade balance

In 2020Q2 export credits fell USD 1,229.69m year on year. Travel credits accounted for USD 848.78m of that decline. Import debits fell USD 925.26m, leaving a USD 304.43m deterioration in the goods-and-services balance. Travel is included in exports. BOP credits and debits measure transactions on an accrual basis and need not coincide with monthly currency conversions.

Banks incurred USD 210.69m in new external loan liabilities **net** in Q2. General government incurred USD 400.10m net external loan liabilities, including USD 200.67m of IMF credit. The government IMF component is already in that total. In Q4 the BOP instead allocates USD 114.11m of net IMF credit to the central bank. Across 2020 the IMF cash ledger records two EFF drawings, SDR 147m on 5 May and SDR 79m on 18 December. Neither record proves that all proceeds remained available for intervention. [Sector and reserve bridge](quarterly.csv) · [IMF approval and budget-support purpose in May](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2020/english/1geoea2020001.pdf)

NBG made USD 916.07m in net market sales in 2020, while gross reserve stocks increased USD 404.90m over the year. In Q2 alone gross reserves rose USD 215.57m, of which USD 189.68m was BOP reserve transactions. The difference of USD 25.89m combines valuation, other changes and possible vintage differences. Repo, swap and other financing positions cannot be added to spot sales without reconciling both legs. The quarter-level analytical BOP balances in all six quarters using its published sign convention: reserve transactions equal IMF credit plus exceptional financing minus the analytical overall balance. This identity describes financing across all sectors; removing the IMF entry while holding every other entry fixed would be a scenario.

## Evidence boundaries and replication

Monthly NBG data use the five-source screen's closing-date conventions. For 2020 the monthly intervention series is net sales positive, opposite the sign in the [locked R3 monthly ledger](../../../data/derived/r3_fx_response/monthly.csv). Dated auction rows are a subset. For 2009 the all-market quarterly disclosures remain at native frequency. USD-equivalent deposits still include non-USD valuation effects. Private bank-level buffer usability, other gross debt maturities, missed or restructured payments and the full Treasury hard-currency cash calendar remain unresolved. Interest and IMF charges are outside the principal ledger. Do not use the records to set a minimum reserve level.

Detailed BOP sheet BOP–BPM6 rows 8/9 and 146 supply exports/imports and travel credits; rows 711–722 identify loan incurrence by sector and rows 712 and 719 its IMF components. Analytical sheet BOP-anlt rows 51–55 supply overall balance, financing, reserve transactions, IMF credit and exceptional financing. Quarterly stocks and flows have separate columns. Monthly [NBG source files](../../../data/source_manifest.json) and dated IMF transactions have byte locks. The [IMF source register](imf_sources.json) records both source URLs.
