## Access and remaining limits

Original raw files remain outside Git. A clone contains every derived output and can verify output integrity/accounting; full extraction requires the pinned original bytes. Exact URLs, sizes and SHA-256 are provided per source. The old 1 MB limit no longer prevents retrieval of ordinary workbooks. If a provider has revised a URL's contents, the hash must fail: seek a lawful original-vintage copy or create a separately registered new vintage. Do not substitute current bytes silently. Rights for republication of original provider documents have not been established, and this checkpoint does not redistribute them.

The isolated-checkout test uses the existing installed environment and local source copies, not another researcher's independently acquired data. That remaining durability/access risk belongs in the R7 independent-release check; it does not invalidate the recorded local reproduction. The source-validation exceptions remain exceptions: dated auction/month alignment, one securities total and transfer-workbook agreement. Historical annual and FX-market discrepancies also remain explicit. Tests do not resolve those economic definitions.
