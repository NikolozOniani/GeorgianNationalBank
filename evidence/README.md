# Existing evidence and admissible uses

This catalogue serves the fixed question in [the research protocol](../research/PROTOCOL.md). All linked files are preserved supporting evidence from commit `46fdde7dd8cb673378154c099b72e8c40060b684`. Their historical titles, sample choices and handoffs are not active instructions.

| Existing evidence | Verified coverage | What it supports | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|---|

The household pilot, credit analyses, earlier illustrations, Phase 1 materials and GEOSTAT release metadata remain in the archive for preservation. They are not active study components and will not be turned into additional chapters. Raw provider files remain untracked with their source hashes; their availability on a fresh checkout depends on provider access or an authorized local copy.

No new empirical estimates were produced during the reorganization. See [verification instructions](../replication/README.md) for the unchanged numerical tests and hash checks.
