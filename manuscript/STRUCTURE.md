# The Economics of Reserve Accumulation in Georgia: An Independent Assessment of Costs, Benefits, and Optimal Reserve Adequacy

**Author: Nikoloz Oniani**

**Research question:** Did the National Bank of Georgia's large-scale foreign-currency purchases prevent the Georgian lari from appreciating, and were the resulting economic costs justified by the benefits of reserve accumulation?

**Scope:** One intervention episode, one economic counterfactual, and its consequences.

This is a structure for the eventual paper, not a manuscript draft. Its argument follows the [single research protocol](../research/PROTOCOL.md).

| Section | Economic purpose | Essential evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Introduction | State the question, contribution and conclusions at their supported strength | The completed episode and counterfactual results |
| 2. The reserve-accumulation episode | Establish the relevant institutional setting, transactions and competing explanations | Executions, policy rules, market conditions and dated external flows |
| 3. Economic framework and identification | Define the no-purchases counterfactual, mechanisms, estimands and restrictions | A defensible assignment mechanism or an explicit structural model |
| 4. Data and measurement | Explain economic definitions, sample, measurement limitations and comparability | Reconciled intervention, market and balance-sheet observations |
| 5. Exchange-rate effects | Estimate the lari path without purchases and distinguish temporary from persistent effects | Valid identification or clearly conditional structural estimates, falsification and uncertainty |
| 6. Economic costs | Evaluate incremental financing, opportunity costs and consequences of prevented appreciation | Matched exposures, returns, feasible financing alternatives and incidence |
| 7. Benefits and reserve adequacy | Evaluate liquidity insurance, financing and market-stability benefits; assess Cangul and Abel | Usable reserves, drains, model replication and sensitivity without duplicated benefits |
| 8. Joint assessment and robustness | Compare the same two paths on a common horizon, numeraire and risk basis | Coherent costs/benefits, uncertainty propagation and break-even conditions |
| 9. Conclusion | Answer the fixed question and state what the evidence does and does not establish | Results from the preceding sections; no new assertions |

Proposed core exhibits are an episode timeline, an intervention/reserve accounting table, the observed and counterfactual exchange-rate paths, an incremental cost account, and a reserve-benefit/break-even comparison. These are planned exhibits, not claims that the estimates already exist.

Economic equations, identification assumptions, variable definitions, sampling and robustness belong in the main text or academic appendices. File paths, GitHub commentary, software commands, data-pipeline implementation and project-management updates belong exclusively in the separate replication documentation. The eventual paper should use neutral language, consistent units, source notes and explicit uncertainty, and distinguish measured facts from causal estimates and conditional simulations.

Earlier manuscripts are superseded and are not chapters of this paper. Do not assemble them into the new structure or retain their conclusions without fresh relevance and validity checks.
